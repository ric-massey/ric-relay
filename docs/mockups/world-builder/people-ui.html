The roles, written down

Text — ink, quieter, a control or link. A need now — going hungry. Lasting nature — stubborn, warm. In mind — heard, believed. An estimate — 3 days of food; a fact is plain: 50 people.

On the map — selected: a solid white ring; followed: a dashed ring in this colour; a standing problem: a ring and a word where it is.

Factions — ABCDEFGH Okabe–Ito, with a letter on each village and in every list, so a ninth people and beyond are told apart by more than colour. A colour is never given to a new people while an old one holding it is alive, unless all eight are.
