# Polish audit — 2026-10-15

Companion to the two 2026-07-31 audits. Those asked "is anything broken?" This one
asks the question that matters now: **where can a visitor still see the seams?**

Every page was opened from a local server at 375px and 1280px and measured, not just
read: horizontal overflow, type under 11px, page weight and the heaviest files,
broken images, images shipped far bigger than they are drawn, and the first screen
as a visitor sees it. A script also checked every local `href`/`src` in all 226 HTML
files, every `<title>`/description/viewport, and every `data-egg` against
`relay-return.js`. Training was checked on the live site too, because locally the
Worker endpoints 404 and the page falls back to committed data.

**The good news first.** No room overflows sideways at 375px. Every egg declared is
defined. Every room except two has a title, a description and a working nav. The
photo lightbox is properly built (arrows, Escape, focus returns). Psyche, Exploration,
Climbing, Training, Apex, CS and the 404 all have strong first screens. The problems
below are seams, not breakage.

What was **not** verified: the browser pane was hidden while this ran, which throttles
lazy-loading and entrance animations, so anything that depends on those (Captures
thumbnails, the Arcade's scroll reveals, the terminal's boot timing) was judged from
the code and the DOM rather than from watching it play. Worker-down states were only
seen locally (where every Worker call fails), not forced on the live site.

Severity: **A** = a visitor notices · **B** = worth a pass · **C** = polish.

## A — the seams a visitor sees

### A1. Kondrite tells three different stories about what it is
The game is now **Survey** — "There is one game, and it is Survey," says its own
README — and its title screen is *one pilot, one chart / BEGIN THE SURVEY*. But:

| Where | What it says Kondrite is |
|---|---|
| `gaming.html` hero, line ~459 | "Up to five ships… co-op survival, or a battle royale… goes online when you want a crowd" · meta *1–5 players · Co-op & Battle Royale · Online* |
| `gaming.html` cover clip `assets/games/kondrite.webm` | asteroid **WAVE 1** footage from the old co-op mode |
| `gaming.html` `og:image:alt` | "a vector ship among drifting asteroids" |
| `latest.js` (the front-door banner) | "KONDRITE, now with a three-mission campaign" |
| `projects/kondrite/index.html` hidden description | Survival vs Battle Royale, A/D/W keys, five colours |
| `projects/kondrite/README.md` first paragraph | co-op, BR, Campaign, *or* Survey |
| `README.md` project table | 1–5 ships, three modes, Campaign |
| the page itself | Survey, alone |

A visitor clicks "Play Kondrite" expecting a party game and gets a solo exploration
game. **Fix:** decide the one sentence ("a survey of endless space — chart it,
salvage it, go further"), then rewrite all eight places to it, re-shoot the cover
art and clip *in Survey*, and give the Arcade card a Survey screenshot. This is the
single biggest polish win on the site.

### A2. The front door hides the rooms below the fold — on desktop too
At 1280×800 the room directory (`#dir`) starts at **y = 1090** — below the first
screen. At 375px it's further still. The first screen is boot text, a dim
"This is the terminal! You start here! Scroll down to see the main different places
you can visit!" and an empty prompt. The line asking the visitor to scroll is the
seam: the page knows the good part is out of reach. **Fix:** shorten the boot block
or let the directory sit beside it on wide screens, so at least the first three
rooms are on screen when the boot finishes; then delete the "scroll down" line.

### A3. Orrin opens with an apology
The first thing on `/orrin` — above the room's own title — is a pink HEADS UP box:
*"This page is generated by an LLM. Ric hasn't had time to read through it and
correct information that may be incorrect."* It is honest, and it is the loudest
"unfinished" signal on the whole site, on the one project hard rule 2 says is the
featured one. **Fix:** read the page once, correct what's wrong, and remove the box —
or, if that can't happen yet, move it to a small footnote under the tour.

### A4. The Entertainment nav doesn't collapse on phones
`entertainment.html` and `entertainment-library.html` at 375px show the
`.roomnav-toggle` ("entertainment" pill) **and** the nav itself as a 70px-wide
sideways-scrolling strip beside it ("terminal c…" clipped). Every other room
collapses the nav behind its toggle, as AGENTS.md requires. Measured: nav
`scrollWidth 737 / clientWidth 70`, `overflow-x: auto`, toggle `display: block`.

### A5. The coolest systems can't be found
`cs.html` — 2D replays with facing, kill snapshots, a radar per map, head-to-head,
match stories — is the most elaborate thing on the site, and **no page links to it**.
Neither does anything link to `val.html` or `apex.html`. The only way in is to know
to type `cs` at the terminal. The unlisting rule exists for *thin* rooms; CS is not
thin. **Decide:** list CS (on the front door, or as a card in Gaming or
Entertainment — wherever "games I play" belongs), or leave it hidden deliberately and
say so in AGENTS.md. VAL should stay hidden until it has data (see B1).

### A6. Photos are shipped at 1600px as thumbnails, and the climbing cat weighs 70 MB
- **Captures** draws 1,163 frames, each the full `photos/website-*.jpg` (1600×1600,
  ~300–870 KB) shown at ~340px wide on a phone. `loading="lazy"` helps, but a phone
  scrolling the sheet pulls ~5–10× the bytes it needs, and the page is **303,000 px
  tall** on a phone with no way to jump by date. **Fix:** generate ~480px thumbnails
  (`photos/thumbs/`), keep the 1600 for the lightbox; add a year/month jump.
- **Mochi's climbing sprites** (`assets/cat/relay-cat-climb-*.png`) are 1024×1536
  PNGs at 0.9–2.4 MB each, 70 MB for the set, and `effects.js` preloads seven poses
  on `climbing.html`. He's drawn at a fraction of that size. **Fix:** downscale to
  ~3× the drawn height and convert to WebP; expect 95%+ off.
- **How Speed Affects Time** loads 4.7 MB before it can play: `milky-way-eso.jpg`
  2.8 MB, `forest-silhouette.png` 2600px wide drawn at 375px.

## B — worth a pass

### B1. Stale or empty truth
- **VALORANT has zero matches** (`val-data.js` is `{"matches":[]}`), so `/val` is an
  entire room of dashes and "No matches on record yet". It also links three times to
  `projects/val/llm/index.md`, which doesn't exist (only the export writes it).
- **`latest.js`** — the front door's one announcement — newest entry is Studies
  (2026-10-03). CS's whole room, Kondrite becoming Survey and the visitor counter
  aren't announced; the Arcade entry still promises a three-mission campaign.
- **The Log** promises "write-ups, trip reports, **and Apex VOD reviews**" in its
  subtitle, description and og tags. It has three entries, newest 2026-07-23, and no
  VOD review.
- **FARLIGHT's `<title>` is "FARLIGHT — feel prototype"** — fine on the Workbench,
  odd on a storefront that sells it as a game.
- **Workbench** greets you with "Nothing here is finished" and holds one item and an
  empty slot. Accurate, but it's a listed room that reads as a placeholder.
- `projects/crossfire/index.html` (the old-name redirect) has no viewport or
  description — invisible to visitors, but a phone hitting an old link gets a
  desktop-zoomed flash.

### B2. Type under the 11px floor
AGENTS.md: nothing sets type below 11px. Measured on screen:
- `gaming.html`: the top strip (9.9px), the ARCADE mark (8.8px), the toggle (10.6px)
  and the nav links (10.9px).
- `cs.html` has a 9px rule in its CSS.
- `apex.html` has 10.5px.
- Inside the projects (the rule is softer there, but these are HUD labels people read
  mid-game): Interstate 40's HUD at **8.5px**, Starfield's flight HUD at 8.8px,
  FARLIGHT's controls at 9px, How Speed Affects Time down to 8.3px, How Big
  Everything Is at 9.3px, Kondrite's settings `DEVICE` label at 8.3px.

### B3. Error states fall back silently to old data
When the Worker is unreachable, Training shows the committed history — locally that
meant "LAST WORKOUT · Tuesday, Aug 18" two months on, under "THIS WEEK 0 of 14".
Falling back is right (AGENTS.md says so); doing it *silently* is the seam. A visitor
can't tell "Ric hasn't trained since August" from "the feed is down". **Fix:** one
quiet line in each room's own voice when a live fetch fails ("feed offline — showing
the last sync, Oct 3"). Same for Climbing's web trips and Entertainment's writes.

### B4. Inconsistent ways back
- The floating "← Ric's Terminal" pill appears on Climbing's **Log** and **Boards**
  pages but not on Media, Stats or To-do, though all five share the same section bar.
  Pick one.
- On phones the pill sits over content at bottom-left (it covers the suggestion
  chips on Studies). Content needs ~56px of bottom padding where the pill is.
- Kondrite has no pill on purpose (thumbs), but its EXIT GAME lives inside Settings —
  and on a portrait phone the first screen is a full-screen "Turn your phone
  sideways" with no way out at all. Give that screen a small "← back to the arcade".

### B5. Small copy and layout seams
- Training (live), the last workout's details run together:
  **"Duration 8 hLoad High — …forerunningRecovery Treat as a hard session"** — the
  label/value pairs have no separators.
- Training's footer shows a bare **Password / Sign in** form to every visitor.
  Owner sign-in belongs behind something quieter — a lock glyph, or a link that
  opens the form.
- `workbench.html` line 195 still carries the authoring hint `href="..."` inside an
  HTML comment — harmless, but the link-checker flags it every time.
- Captures and Entertainment each have a bare `<img>` in their markup with no `alt`
  (Captures' rendered frames do get `alt=""`, which is right with a caption under
  them — give the static tag the same).
- `atlas/index.html`, `autism-reflection.html` and the five `spacetime/` pages have
  no meta description (link previews show nothing).

### B6. External fonts on two rooms
`climbing.html` and `gaming.html` load Google Fonts (Archivo/Newsreader, Poppins).
Hard rule 4 says no external fonts; it also means a font swap on first paint and a
third-party request on every visit. Self-host the woff2s next to the room (the
climbing section already has `assets/climbing/`).

## C — polish

- **Captures:** phone Back closes the page, not the open photo. Push a history entry
  when the lightbox opens so Back closes it.
- **Arcade hero on a phone:** the cover clip is near-black vector footage, so for the
  first moments (before the text animates in) the screen reads as empty. A slightly
  brighter poster frame, or the title rendered without the delay, fixes the first
  10 seconds.
- **Training Workouts** is 19,600px tall on a phone — every session of the plan.
  Collapse past weeks.
- **`/psyche` vs `/mind`** — the known drift from AGENTS.md is still there: the front
  door says `/mind`, every nav says `psyche`.
- `projects/offramp/data/i40.js` is 900 KB and loads before the first frame —
  expected for a real corridor, but it's the one game with a blank wait; a "loading
  the interstate…" line would cover it.

## Suggested order

1. **A1 Kondrite's story** — one sentence, eight places, new cover and clip.
2. **A2 + A3** — the two first screens: terminal fold and Orrin's banner.
3. **A4** — the Entertainment nav on phones (small, CSS only).
4. **A6** — thumbnails for Captures, a downsized Mochi, How Speed's 4.7 MB.
5. **A5** — decide CS's front door.
6. **B1–B3** — `latest.js`, the Log, VAL, the silent fallbacks.
7. Everything else as a sweep.

None of this needs a new feature, and none of it should be fixed by making rooms
look more like each other.
