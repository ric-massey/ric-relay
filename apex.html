How this gets read

This page reads Apex three different ways, because three different kinds of number live in three different places.

Matches come from the game itself. Respawn ship a LiveAPI — a first-party event stream, switched on with a launch option, that pushes every hit, knock, kill and placement to a listener on this machine as it happens. Those numbers are exact: they are Apex's own state, not a reading of it. The placement arrives at the moment the squad goes down, which is why a match is usually on this page before the summary screen has faded.

Per-legend totals come through the keyhole. Lifetime tracker values are not gameplay events, so LiveAPI never mentions them — the only public source is the three trackers equipped on the banner of the legend being played. So this half of the page isn't a logbook, it's an accumulator: pull-apex.py takes a daily snapshot, keeps the highest number it has ever seen, and never lets one go down. A legend with no tracker on it has, as far as the internet is concerned, never been played.

Career totals are read off the screen. The account-wide figure Apex shows when you click your gamertag in the lobby is on no API of any kind — not LiveAPI, not the community ones. It is read by OCR when that panel happens to be open, or typed in by hand, and either way it is stamped with the date it was read and shown with a ~.
