coffee3's MLS-MPM transfer equations are adapted from Splash:
https://github.com/matsuoka-601/Splash
The original demo and its documentation made the live 3D approach concrete.

Algorithm references:
- Hu et al., A Moving Least Squares Material Point Method with Displacement
  Discontinuity and Two-Way Rigid Body Coupling, SIGGRAPH 2018.
  https://yuanming.taichi.graphics/publication/2018-mlsmpm/
- nialltl, Incremental MPM (density recomputed each step):
  https://nialltl.neocities.org/articles/mpm_guide
- Sigg and Hadwiger, Fast Third-Order Texture Filtering, GPU Gems 2, 2005:
  https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems2/part-iii-high-quality-rendering/chapter-20-fast-third-order-texture-filtering
  Eight trilinear lookups implement cubic B-spline density reconstruction.
  The volume renderer is specific to this shallow fixed view; it does not
  use Splash's screen-space renderer. No environment images are copied.
