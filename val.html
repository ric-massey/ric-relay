Where this comes from

Riot's own match data. After each game the VALORANT client keeps a full record of the match: every kill, where every player was standing when it happened and which way they were facing, the damage each player did to each other player, every buy, and the spike. This room reads that record from the game on Ric's computer while he plays.

Exact positions. Every position is the game's own coordinates, drawn on the map's real minimap and labelled with Riot's callout names: the same picture as the timeline in the game's match report.

Other players are shown by agent. VALORANT stat sites only name players who opted in, so this room does the same.

Plain text too. All of it, every fight with every coordinate included, is written out for anything that reads the site without running it: the full record, a report per match, and a callout map for each map.
