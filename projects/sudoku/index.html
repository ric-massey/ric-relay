Sudoku

Scoreboard

    Good game.

    1–3 letters. Shared with everyone on this board.

    How to play

    Two ways in. Tap a square, then a number. Or tap a number first (with no square chosen) and then every square it goes in; tap the number again to put it down. Tap a chosen square again to let go of it.

    Notes. Turn on Notes to pencil in small candidates. Placing a number clears it from the notes in its row, column and box.

    Expert puzzles can't be finished by filling in squares with only one possibility: you'll need pairs and the like.
