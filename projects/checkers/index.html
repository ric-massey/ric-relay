    How to play

    The rules are beside the board. Tap a piece and then a square, or drag it. When a jump is available it has to be taken, and the pieces that can jump glow. Choose a style above; changing it keeps your game in place.

    Your games are saved in this browser, so you can leave and come back. Undo takes back your last turn; Hint suggests a move.
