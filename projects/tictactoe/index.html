    Good game.

    1–3 letters. Shared with everyone on this board.

    How to play

    Ultimate. Nine small boards make one big one. The square you play in sends the other player to the small board in that same position; if that board is already decided, they can play anywhere. Win a small board with three in a row there; win the game with three small boards in a row.

    Matches. Play single games, best of 3 or best of 5 (first to 2 or 3 wins; draws are replayed). Who starts swaps every game, so the computer starts every other one.

    Online. Pick "Play a friend online", start a game and send the link. Classic or Ultimate, whichever was chosen when you started it.
