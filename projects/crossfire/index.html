CROSSFIRE — Asteroids in three modes

A local multiplayer game for one keyboard. Survival puts every ship on the same side against a field of drifting rocks, sharing one pool of lives, with friendly fire on — a shot that hits your partner destroys them and costs the team a life. Survival Safe is the same game with shots passing harmlessly through your partner. Battle Royale is a larger arena where every ship is an enemy, each has three lives, and the arena wall closes inward until only one ship is left flying, around stationary stars and black holes that pull ships, rocks and shots towards them. In the survival modes the field wraps: fly off one edge and you arrive at the opposite one, and so do the rocks and the shots. Battle royale has solid walls instead — ships and rocks bounce off them, bullets are destroyed by them — and those walls close inward as the match runs on. One or two people play at the same keyboard, one player per phone, and up to five play online with a screen each. Each player is named for the colour of their ship. Amber steers with A and D, thrusts with W and fires with the space bar. Green, the second player at a keyboard, steers with the left and right arrow keys, thrusts with the up arrow and fires with Enter. Cyan, pink and violet are flown by bots or by other people online. Those are the keys it starts with: press C at the title screen to open the controls, where every key can be reassigned by choosing it and pressing the key you want, and the browser remembers your choices. Press P to pause, and Tab in battle royale to follow a different ship. This game requires a keyboard.
