    How to play

    Three games. Marathon goes on until the blocks reach the top, faster every ten lines. Sprint: clear 40 lines as fast as you can. Timed: as many points as you can in two minutes.

    The ghost. The outline at the bottom shows where the falling piece will land. Turn it off in Controls.

    Controls. Set how long a held key waits before it repeats and how fast it repeats, and change any key. On a phone: drag to move, tap to turn, flick down to drop, or use the buttons.
