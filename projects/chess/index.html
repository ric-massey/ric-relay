    How to play

    Moving. Tap a piece, then a marked square, or drag it. Castle by moving the king two squares. A pawn reaching the far rank asks what it becomes.

    Looking back. Tap any move on the score sheet, or use ← →, to see the board at that point. Tap the board to come back.

    Drawing on the board. Right-drag from one square to another for an arrow, right-click a square for a ring. Your next move clears them.

    Who you play. The computer (three levels), a friend on this device (pass it across, or sit opposite with the board between you), or a friend online: start a game and send them the link.

    Terminal. Type moves at the prompt: e4, Nf3, e7e5, O-O. Type help for the commands.

    Your game is saved in this browser. Changing the set keeps the game in place.
