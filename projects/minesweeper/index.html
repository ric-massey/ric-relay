    How to play

    Flags. Right-click, press and hold, or turn on Flag mode, to flag a square you think is a mine.

    Question marks. Turn on ? mode, or Shift+right-click (or middle-click) on a computer, for a square you're unsure about. A question mark is only a note: it doesn't count as a flag, and you can still open the square.

    Opening around a number. Once a number has all its flags, click it to open every other square around it.

    Custom size. Pick "Custom size" for your own rows, columns and mines. Custom games stay off the scoreboard.
