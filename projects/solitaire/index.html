    How to play

    Moving. Drag a card or a run of cards, or tap it and then where it goes. Tap a card twice (or double-click) to send it where it fits, home first. Tap the deck to turn cards over.

    Points. 5 for moving a card from the deck to the table, 10 for each card sent home, 5 for turning a card face up. Taking a card back off a suit pile costs 15, going through the deck again costs 100 (20 when drawing three). Points never go below zero.

    Winnable deals. With it on, a solver plays each deal out before you see it and only deals one it can win. It doesn't change the daily deal, which is the same for everyone.

    Your game is saved in this browser. Changing the deck keeps the game in place.
