    1–3 letters. Shared with everyone on this board.

    How to play

    Colours. Each style has its own colours for a letter in the right spot, a letter elsewhere in the word, and one not in it; the line under the keyboard says which. Colour-blind switches every style to orange and blue, with ● and ○ marks on the tiles.

    Past daily words. Pick "Past daily words" and a date to play a day you missed. It counts as that day's game, but stays off the scoreboard.

    Hint. In practice, Hint: a letter shows one right letter in its place. A hinted game stays off the board.
