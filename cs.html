About this data

Valve first. Every Counter-Strike match is recorded by the game itself as a demo: every player's position, every shot and grenade, tick by tick. Each demo is saved and parsed into this page, and that is where the round-by-round history, the weapons and the 2D replay come from.

The live feed. While the game is open it also reports its own state as it plays, so a match lands here the moment the scoreboard says it's over, with the money and loadout for every round.

Leetify fills the gaps. Valve deletes its demos after a few weeks. For games whose demo is already gone, the numbers come from Leetify, who parsed it while it existed, along with the aim and utility measurements only they make. Each match says which of the three it came from.

Plain text too. Everything here, every fight in every round included, is also written out as plain text for anything that reads the site without running it: the full record, with a report per match.

Everyone plays under their in-game name. The other players in a match show up the same way they do on any public stats site.
